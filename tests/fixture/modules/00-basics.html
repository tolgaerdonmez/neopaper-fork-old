Basics

Notation, a derivation, pseudocode and a quiz

Notation

We write the greatest common divisor as $\gcd(a, b)$.

SymbolMeaningDefined in
$a, b$the two inputsSection 1
$a \bmod b$remainder of $a$ divided by $b$Eq. (1)

Key ideaReplacing $(a, b)$ by $(b, a \bmod b)$ never changes the answer.

Derivation

$$\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(b, a \bmod b)$$

Any common divisor of $a$ and $b$ divides $a - qb$.

$$\gcd(a, 0) = a$$

Everything divides $0$.

Pseudocode, controls as next sibling (upstream layout)

ALGORITHM WHILE $b \neq 0$ $(a, b) \gets (b, a \bmod b)$ RETURN $a$
EXPLANATION

Stop once the remainder is zero.

Shift and take the remainder.

The last non-zero value is the answer.
